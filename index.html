Join us for our second edition!

After a first October Big Day in 2025, this year moves to the afternoon. We start with lunch and bird-themed activities at Fort Canning Centre, then hear from experienced birders and scientists on avian research, conservation and citizen science through eBird.

From 4 pm, everyone heads out to one of six sites across the island. Each group is led by ornithologists, experienced birders and youth volunteers, with at least one BirdSoc SG site leader present. Transportation from Fort Canning Centre to the sites is provided.

Not registering? You can still take part in eBird's October Big Day. Anyone in Singapore can submit a checklist on 10 October, wherever you are — let's make it the day with the most eBird checklists ever submitted in Singapore!

Why join October Big Day?

Citizen science

Every sighting goes into eBird, where it informs studies and conservation policy here and abroad.

Local biodiversity

Six sites in one afternoon show how much birdlife our parks and nature spaces hold — we're likely to record both migratory and resident species.

Community

Experienced birders, scientists, and newer birders can all learn from one another, and grow the pool of people contributing to science here.

What is eBird?

eBird is the world's largest citizen-science platform for birds — over two billion sightings from more than a million birders, run by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. You log a checklist of what you saw, where and when, and eBird keeps it forever. It's always free, with a mobile app and website.

For you

Your birding, made easier

A tidy, searchable record of your birding — your life list, your patch, your year. Find your target species by seeing exactly where and when others are reporting them, and plan trips with hotspot maps and recent-sighting alerts.

For birds & science

Evidence for conservation

Those same checklists join two billion others into the single largest dataset on bird distribution and abundance ever assembled — letting researchers model ranges, migration and abundance, and grounding conservation decisions in trends only large-scale data can reveal.

Singapore's country partner

Since 2025, the Bird Society of Singapore has been eBird's official country partner for Singapore — keeping the island's records accurate and helping more birders get started. Every checklist you log on 10 October counts towards the global October Big Day.
